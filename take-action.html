How it works

Pick a campaign

Each campaign has one clear ask, the facts behind it, and a letter that is ready to send.

Enter your address

We look up your districts and pull your officials — members of Congress from the official legislators list, state and local officials from the MAPAC directory where available.

Review and send

Your name and city fill in automatically. Edit the letter if you like, then send it from your own email app. A copy goes to MAPAC when you say yes.

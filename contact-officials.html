Contact your elected officials

Pick your offices, write your message, find your officials by address, and send it — all in one place, no link or account needed.

    Who do you want to contact?

    Pick every office you want to email — federal executive, Congress, state, and local. One message goes to each of them, personalized with their name.

    What do you want to say?

    Start from a thank-you or an ask, pick a topic, then use Enhance to fill in the blanks. You can still edit every word before sending.

    Built-in suggestions — no account or AI service needed. Pick a topic that matches your message, then Enhance.

    Placeholders: {title} {last} {office} are the official; {name} {city} are you — they are filled in automatically for each official.

    Find your officials

    Enter your home address. We use it to find your districts and officials. Elected officials only respond to their own constituents.

    Privacy: information you provide on this page may be collected by MAPAC for security and follow-up purposes.

    Your message preview

    Your officials

    Uncheck anyone you do not want to contact. Some officials only accept messages through a web form — we show you exactly how to reach them.

    Review and edit your message

    Add a personal story: it is the most effective thing you can do. Your name and contact details are added below each message.

    Built-in suggestions — no account or AI service needed.

    Send your message

    Each button opens your own email app with the message ready. Press Send there. For officials with no public email, one click copies your message and opens their official contact form — paste it in and send.

      A FACTUAL COMPARISON

      MAPAC is backing Senator Chris Van Hollen for Senate Democratic Leader. The public record below, side by side, is why — read it, then make your voice heard.

      CHRIS VAN HOLLEN

      U.S. Senator, Maryland

      🤝
      AIPAC:

      In a January 2026 interview, said: “I don’t accept AIPAC money” and said he did not think anyone should accept it.

      ⚖
      Israel aid:

      Has advocated conditions on U.S. military assistance and opposed transfers of offensive weapons while Israel violates international law; distinguish offensive from defensive aid. He voted for a $14 billion Israel security assistance package in February 2024.

      🕊
      Gaza:

      Said the “overwhelming evidence” points to genocide, while clarifying he had not made an independent legal determination.

      🛡
      Immigration:

      Defended due process in the Kilmar Abrego García case and challenged the administration’s handling of his deportation and ICE custody.

      📅
      First 100 days:

      Called for making healthcare more affordable, restoring Medicaid funding, extending ACA tax credits, and establishing a livable minimum wage.

      ⚠ READ THE RECORD CAREFULLY

      Absence of a statement in this review does not prove a senator has never made one. Van Hollen’s record includes support for some Israeli security assistance; his stated distinction is between offensive and defensive weapons.
